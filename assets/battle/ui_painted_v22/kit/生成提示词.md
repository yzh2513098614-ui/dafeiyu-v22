# 战斗界面素材生成

模式：内置图像生成。两张透明素材图集用于最新 v22 的战斗界面。参考图仅用于颜色和材质，不替换卡牌插画。

## 面板与按钮

保存：panels_and_buttons.png

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: Production TRANSPARENT game UI atlas. Reference image is STYLE ONLY: match the matte hand-painted cream rim, subtle paper texture and muted color plaques of its cards. Do not reproduce cards.
Primary request: make the REST of this cute seaside deckbuilding RPG battle UI equally polished. Nine usable blank UI components, strict 3 columns x 3 rows on a square canvas, equal cells, all centered in their cell with at least 30px transparent gutter. Face-on, no perspective. Professional functional interface materials with layered ivory ceramic/paper rims, fine slate-blue outline, delicate soft bevel and restrained surface grain.
Row 1 left: wide rounded cream paper information panel, aspect 3:1, gently curved corner shoulders and a slim blue-gray outer rim. Large clean blank center.
Row 1 center: wide pale sage-green hand tray, aspect 3:1, recessed center and rounded cream rim. Entire face is solid, no holes.
Row 1 right: wide warm ivory player status tray, aspect 3:1, smooth cream center, double shallow ivory rim and slate edge.
Row 2 left: wide sky-blue-cream title plaque, aspect 3:1, subtle curved top and bottom silhouette, rim like the cards but lighter.
Row 2 center: almost square ivory ENERGY tile, aspect 1.2:1, seafoam inner rim and very pale sage face. Blank spacious center, no orb or symbol.
Row 2 right: wide PRIMARY action button, aspect 2.5:1, muted peach-coral face, ivory rim, gentle raised surface. A clear blank center.
Row 3 left: wide SECONDARY action button, aspect 2.5:1, pale mint face and ivory rim.
Row 3 center: wide TARGET action button, aspect 2.5:1, pale lavender-rose face and ivory rim.
Row 3 right: small cream label/tab plaque, aspect 2.5:1, pale blue-gray inset rim.
Constraints: real transparent background outside components. NO text, NO digits, NO icons, NO characters, NO checkerboard. Corners and ends may have depth; central and straight-edge areas must be clean and stretchable for nine-slice UI. Soft enough for cartoon game, detailed enough to feel crafted, not flat vector wireframe. NO flowers, pearls, shells, vines, bows, stars, badges, center diamonds, rope, filigree, protruding ornaments. No drop shadow outside component. Keep consistent line thickness and material finish across all nine.
```

## 进度条、头像框与敌方铭牌

保存：gauges_and_portrait.png

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: transparent battle HUD components texture atlas. Reference image is STYLE REFERENCE only. Match its muted blue-gray outline, layered cream rim, soft hand-painted matte finish. Create FOUR UI parts, centered in a strict 2x2 equal-cell grid on a square transparent canvas, plenty of transparent spacing.
Top left: EMPTY horizontal progress track, aspect 7:1, capsule ends, narrow ivory ceramic rim, thin slate-blue outer outline, shallow pale blue-gray recessed center. This is an empty track only, no filled colored portion.
Top right: WHITE-to-light-GRAY neutral glossy progress FILL, aspect 7:1, capsule ends, no external rim. Grayscale neutral white satin material, a restrained upper highlight and faint lower shading. Intended to be dynamically color tinted pink, mint or blue by the game. MUST NOT contain a colored track or outline.
Bottom left: SQUARE avatar portrait FRAME, aspect 1:1, generous truly TRANSPARENT center hole occupying the inner 75% of the square. Rounded cream rim, fine slate outer contour and mint inner edge. No portrait inside it.
Bottom right: wide enemy NAME PLATE, aspect 5:1, pale ivory face, narrow sage rim and fine slate-blue edge. Spacious blank center.
Constraints: genuine alpha outside every component, plus inside portrait frame. NO text, digits, icons, symbols, checker pattern, characters, stars, shells, pearls, bows, floral ornaments, center decorations. Face-on, consistent production UI look, stretchable straight edges and flat clean centers. Thin enough to work at 24px height progress bars. No perspective or cast shadows.
```

素材的使用区域记录在 regions.json。运行时保留边角比例，根据控件尺寸伸展中间区域。数值、文字、图标、血量、按钮状态独立显示。
