# 战斗卡框精修

使用内置图像生成工具，生成六种功能配色的透明卡框；技能插画继续使用已完成的 42 张独立插画。

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: production game UI texture atlas, SIX EMPTY CARD FRAMES on a true transparent background, no background checker pattern.
Primary request: polished charming seaside deckbuilding RPG cards. The previous frames were either excessively decorated or cheap flat rectangles. Aim for thoughtfully shaped, tactile, restrained premium cartoon card frames. Let silhouette, rim thickness, matte material shading and functional typography areas create sophistication. NO actual text or digits. NO illustrations inside the art windows.
Composition: square 1536x1536 canvas, strict 3 columns by 2 rows, each identical portrait card occupies exactly one equal grid cell. All six aligned in identical geometry, generous transparent gutter. Card aspect 2:3. Face-on flat UI, zero perspective. Every card has softly clipped shoulders, rounded lower corners, a thick layered ivory rim with a dark desaturated blue fine outline and a delicate edge highlight. Inside is a square TRANSPARENT artwork window at x=12%..88%, y=20%..70%. At top, a shaped colored title plaque occupies x=28%..94%, y=4%..18%: shallow curved ends and a single thin cream accent contour, slightly inset, enough blank space for a skill name. A cream ceramic circular energy medallion at x=5%..28%, y=3%..18% with colored inner ring and subtle depth, EMPTY. Bottom effect area x=6%..94%, y=72%..96%: warm pale cream matte paper with subtle soft grain, ample uninterrupted blank center. A very small functional category diamond centered on the seam between artwork and description, integrated into rim, no other decorations.
Palette: top row coral attack, ocean blue defense, sage mint healing; bottom row lavender control, slate teal strategy, honey energy. Medium muted color values, soft hand-painted cartoon surface finish, cream outlines, gray-blue shadows. Category color mostly title and narrow inner art rim. Outer card cream.
Lighting/material: subtle soft bevels, restrained layered paper/ceramic impression, not metallic gold, no shiny plastic, no photorealistic surface.
Constraints: genuine alpha OUTSIDE card and INSIDE square art aperture; all text areas solid and blank. Consistent geometry across all six. High visual polish with usable negative space. NO flowers, pearls, seashell clusters, bows, ropes, vines, stars, gems, filigree, excessive ornaments. NO plain flat wireframes. NO words, no text labels, no numbers, no watermark. No cast shadow extending past card. Transparent gutters.
```

参考研究：Mega Crit 开发者 Casey Yano 的 GDC 2019 演讲中，卡牌用费用、标题、插画和效果区形成稳定的信息顺序。这里沿用这个信息顺序，造型、颜色和素材重新设计。
